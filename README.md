# Adventure Works Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Excel to analyze Adventure Works sales data and understand product performance, regional sales, production costs, and monthly sales trends through an interactive dashboard.

## Project Objectives
- Monitor total sales, total profit, and production costs.
- Compare sales across product categories and regions.
- Analyze quarterly and monthly sales performance.
- Identify the top five products by sales.
- Explore sales using Year, Country, and Territory filters.

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Production Cost, and Total Profit.
- **Category Analysis:** Sales across Accessories, Bikes, and Clothing.
- **Quarterly Analysis:** Sales distribution across Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.
- **Monthly Trend:** Month-wise sales performance.
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales across countries and regions.
- **Top Products:** Five leading products by sales.
- **Interactive Filters:** Year, Country, and Territory.

## Key KPIs and Insights
- Total Sales: 29.36M.
- Total Production Cost: 17.28M.
- Total Profit: 12.08M.
- Bikes are the largest displayed product category, with sales of approximately 28.32M.
- The displayed quarterly sales values are approximately 9.11M for Q1, 7.09M for Q2, 7.64M for Q3, and 5.52M for Q4.
- The top five products have displayed sales values ranging from approximately 1.29M to 1.36M.
